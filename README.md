# Virtual Assistant Portfolio

## Oakhaven Partners

This repository presents selected work from my Virtual Assistant contract with Oakhaven Partners, a business and management consulting firm, from January 2025 to December 2025.

My responsibilities included administrative coordination, calendar and meeting management, email and file organisation, online research, task tracking, content creation, social media management, competitor research, and short-form video editing.

## Main Portfolio

- `Caleb_Idise_Oakhaven_Partners_Portfolio.pdf` provides an overview of the engagement, responsibilities, workflows, tools, case studies, and professional strengths demonstrated during the contract.

## Supporting Samples

### Weekly Operations and Task Tracker

Demonstrates calendar coordination, meeting preparation, research, reminders, task prioritisation, content work, publishing activities, deadlines, status tracking, and follow-up management.

### Competitor Research Workbook

Demonstrates structured competitor research for the business and management consulting market, including positioning, audiences, services, content approaches, visible strengths, potential gaps, and brand opportunities.
